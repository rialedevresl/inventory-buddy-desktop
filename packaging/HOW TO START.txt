Inventory Buddy Desktop - how to start it
=======================================

You need Python once (it's free and safe, and Windows trusts it):

  1. Go to https://www.python.org/downloads/ and press the yellow
     Download button for Windows.
  2. Run what it downloads and follow the steps (the default choices are fine).

Then:

  3. Right-click the Inventory Buddy Desktop zip you downloaded and choose
     "Extract All...". Put the folder somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents.
  4. Open the folder and double-click "Start Inventory Buddy Desktop".
     The app opens in its own window.
     (If Windows asks which app to open it with, pick Python.)

The first time it starts, the app puts an "Inventory Buddy Desktop" shortcut
with its icon on your desktop, so next time you can start it from there.
(Don't want it? Just delete the shortcut; it won't come back.)

Using it
  - Quit Firestorm first, then press "Start Firestorm" in the app and log in
    as usual. The app never sees your password.
  - Nothing in your inventory changes until you press Run on the Plan & Run
    page, and every run can be undone.
  - Close the window when you're done. The app stops by itself shortly after
    (it stays on while Firestorm is connected, so you can reopen the window).

Your categories and choices are saved in %APPDATA%\SL Inventory Cleanup,
so they carry over when you update to a newer version: just replace this
folder with the new one.
