**Inventory Buddy Desktop 1.0** ♡ an inventory organizer for Second Life®, by Ria Ledevre.

It reads your inventory through Firestorm, helps you sort it into tidy category folders, finds
duplicates, gathers your landmarks and windlights, and only changes anything when you press Run,
with every step checked and an undo. It never sees your password.

**Recommended: the zip** (the Inventory-Buddy-Desktop .zip file below). It needs Python, free from
python.org, installed once. Extract the zip and double-click **Start Inventory Buddy Desktop**.
"HOW TO START.txt" inside walks you through it, and the app puts a shortcut on your desktop.

**Setup .exe (experimental):** a normal installer, but it isn't signed yet (signing certificates
cost a yearly fee that's a lot for one person making a free tool). Windows may show "Windows
protected your PC": press More info, then Run anyway. On PCs with Smart App Control it's blocked
completely. If that happens, the zip works everywhere.

Works with Firestorm on Windows. Something sorted wrong, or an idea? Tell me on Instagram
(@ledeverse) or IM Ria Ledevre in-world. It really helps the sorting get better.
